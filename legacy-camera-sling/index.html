Legacy Camera Sling – Visual Identity, Product Photography & Campaign Assets

Legacy Camera Sling is a branding and visual identity project by Yianni Xilikakis. The work includes product photography, lifestyle imagery, and campaign assets designed to create a cohesive and distinctive brand presence across both digital and physical platforms.

The project demonstrates strategic brand design, considered typography, and creative storytelling tailored to engage its target audience.

overview

A digital launch campaign developed to introduce a new product with clarity and visual authority.

The project included product photography, lifestyle imagery, typographic development, and campaign rollout assets.

Product Photography

product photography

Studio photography emphasizing material precision and brand tone.

Controlled lighting and minimal composition ensured clarity and premium positioning.

Lifestyle Photography

lifestyle

Context-driven imagery positioning the product within daily environments.

The goal was to connect product utility with lived experience and audience relevance.

Campaign Assets & Typography

typography & visual identity

A structured typographic system built for clarity and campaign consistency.

Hierarchy and spacing were refined for use across digital advertising, landing pages, and promotional materials.

outcome

The campaign launched the product with cohesive visual direction and immediate recognition.

Strategic alignment across imagery and typography supported brand positioning and market entry.
